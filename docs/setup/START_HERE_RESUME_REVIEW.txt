🔄 HOW IT WORKS
================================================================================

1. User fills out Q&A
   ↓
2. AI generates resume
   ↓
3. Beautiful modal appears
   ↓
4. User either:
   a) Accepts → Resume saved, URL generated, Done!
   b) Requests rewrite → Types feedback → AI regenerates (up to 5 times)

AI Learning Example:
  V1: "Software engineer with 10 years"
  User: "Add leadership"
  V2: "Technical leader with 10 years"
  User: "Add AWS"
  V3: "Cloud architect with leadership and AWS"
  User: ✓ Perfect! Accept!
